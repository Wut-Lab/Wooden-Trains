# Wooden-Trains
A simple wooden train game with a few other features.

**Choo-Choo Cards** is a side-scrolling wooden toy train game for kids. It's all in one file, `index.html`, so open it in any web browser to play. No install needed.

## How to play
- Tap the screen (or press **Space** / **↑**) to jump.
- Hop over ABC blocks, teddies, bouncy balls and gaps in the track.
- Catch stars as you go. They pile up in the back wagon. Rare **blue stars** are worth 2!
- Rescue the **rubber ducks** waiting on the track: just roll into them. Each one is worth 3 stars and rides along in the back wagon. More ducks bob in the ponds under the gaps.
- Keep an eye out for **special ducks**: 16 rare collectible ducks (Dragon, Zombie, Unicorn, Gold, Cowboy, Robin Hood, Knight, Punk, Chess and more). Each one you find appears in the **Special Ducks** collection on the main menu.
- You have 3 hearts. A bump costs one, and falling in the water makes a splash and bounces you out.

## Modes
- **Journey:** reach the station. Each journey is a little longer.
- **Endless:** see how far you can get. Your best distance is saved.

Speed and difficulty creep up slowly the farther you go, with low caps because it's for kids.

## Unlocks
Stars are saved between games and unlock new things:
- **Track pieces:** every trip is built at random from the pieces you've unlocked (gaps, tunnels, bumpy track, bridges, launch ramps, bouncy balls...).
- **Trains:** seven painted wooden steam engines, including Rainbow and Goldie.
- **Power-up cards:** bring 2 (later 3) on each trip and tap them once: Bubble Shield, Star Magnet, Extra Heart, Turbo Steam, Slow Snail, Rainbow Wings.

Progress is stored in your browser. "Start over" in the Toy Box clears it.

## Controls
| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Jump / start | Tap anywhere | Space, ↑ or W |
| Use a card | Tap the card | 1, 2, 3 |
| Pause | ⏸️ button | P or Esc |
